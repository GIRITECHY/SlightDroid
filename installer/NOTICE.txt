SlightDroid 0.1.0 alpha - an Android emulator for low-end Windows PCs
=================================================================

ALPHA SOFTWARE. Expect rough edges. No ads, no store, no analytics: SlightDroid sends
nothing anywhere.

What Setup does
- Installs SlightDroid to the folder you choose (about 3.9 GB installed).
- Checks this PC. If "Windows Hypervisor Platform" is needed and off, it asks before
  turning it on (this needs a restart). It never changes BIOS settings.
- Adds Start menu and desktop shortcuts, and lets you open .apk files with SlightDroid.
- Your Android data (virtual device, quick-boot snapshot, apps, files) is kept in
  %LOCALAPPDATA%\SlightDroid.
